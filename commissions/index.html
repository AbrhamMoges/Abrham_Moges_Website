HXOUSE Labs World Tour
San Francisco x The Weeknd

Hand-selected to participate in the HXOUSE Labs World Tour in San Francisco, an elite creative technology initiative created in collaboration with The Weeknd and the XO creative team. Chosen among a highly curated group of emerging designers, technologists, and immersive creatives, the experience centered around innovation, collaboration, and the future of digital culture.

As part of the intensive design challenge, I developed a custom 3D visual branding asset for the HXOUSE Labs World Tour, drawing inspiration from the futuristic aesthetic and cinematic atmosphere surrounding The After Hours Til Dawn era. The work combined bold visual storytelling, experimental branding, and immersive presentation design to create a polished promotional piece aligned with the event's high-profile creative direction.

The program provided a rare opportunity to collaborate within a fast-paced creative environment alongside artists, designers, engineers, and strategists, while contributing to concepts tied to one of the most influential global music and visual brands of the decade. This experience strengthened my approach to experiential design, 3D visualization, and culturally driven creative storytelling.

About Me

I'm a creative technologist with a Bachelor of Arts in Information Technology, blending design and development to build engaging digital experiences. My skill set spans across Adobe Creative Cloud, where I create polished visual assets and user-focused designs, as well as Blender and 3D design, where I bring concepts to life through motion, depth, and immersive visuals. Alongside my design background, I have experience in front-end development, allowing me to translate ideas into responsive, functional interfaces that not only look good but perform seamlessly. I approach every project with a balance of creativity and technical precision, aiming to deliver work that is both visually compelling and user-centered.
